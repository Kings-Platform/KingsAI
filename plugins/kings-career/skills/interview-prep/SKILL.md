# Interview prep

The plugin **prepares**; the candidate **performs**. That line is not negotiable: a test or a
screening answered by the AI is misrepresentation, and the profile's rules can't override it.

## 1. What is this?

| Kind | What to find out first |
|---|---|
| Timed technical test (Coodesh, HackerRank, Codility…) | Duration, sections, question formats, monitoring (camera, screen), pause policy, attempts, deadline — from the platform's candidate help page, not from guesses |
| AI screening by chat or audio (DigAÍ, HireVue…) | Number of questions, time per question, attempts, whether it's typed or spoken |
| Recorded intro (audio/video, "2 minutes in English") | Time cap, language, what they assess |
| Human interview | Format (technical, behavioral, system design), who, length |

Read the platform's own help page when there is one, and quote the limits with the link.

## 2. Topics — from the posting

Take the posting's requirements (the card from `job-screener`, or the pipeline row's source)
and turn them into a short list of probable topics, marked **strong / review / gap** against
`facts.md` → Skills. Review and gap items get one line each on what to brush up.

## 3. Stories — from the profile, with numbers

`facts.md` → Stories. Pick the two or three that match the role; for each, the STAR shape in
bullet points the candidate can say in their own words — **not** a text to read aloud. A story
without a number ("cut the code by 40%", "first unit tests in the team") is weaker: ask the
candidate for the number rather than inventing one.

## 4. Scripts for timed answers

For an audio or video: a script under the time cap at a calm pace (~130 words per minute),
in the requested language, opening with name and role, closing with what they're looking for.
Add pronunciation notes only for names and terms the candidate flagged. Suggest rehearsing
twice before recording.

For a live AI screening: a **structure** per expected question (one opening sentence, two
technical points, one concrete example), so the candidate talks, not reads.

## 5. Honesty notes

When the posting asks for something the candidate lacks (a stack, years), prepare the honest
sentence for it — how to say "limited, but here's the adjacent experience" — instead of
coaching around it.

## Output

Topics (strong / review / gap) · stories (bullets) · script or structure · platform rules with
links · what to do before starting (quiet room, example test, time blocked).
