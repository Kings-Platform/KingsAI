# Eightfold

`explore.jobs.<company>…` or `careers.<company>…` with `/careers/apply?pid=<id>`. A single page,
no login.

- **CV upload** opens a privacy-acknowledge modal, then parses the file (~10 s): wait for the
  parse before filling, and re-read what it filled.
- Selecting Brazil as the country adds a required **State** field.
- **Custom comboboxes:** click the control, then click the option's text.
- **Self-ID checkboxes:** coordinate clicks.
